# 🤝 Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
This project welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. Participation should remain respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior
- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not a person's identity or worth.
- Give useful context and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect different levels of experience and communication styles.
- Protect privacy. Do not publish employee, contributor, or visitor personal information.
- Respect third-party creators, licenses, and attribution requirements.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on evidence and project outcomes.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention without consent, retaliation, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Project spaces
This policy applies to repository issues, discussions, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where someone is participating on behalf of the project.

## Reporting a concern
Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact method on the `@brajesh31` GitHub profile. Include links or relevant context and avoid forwarding sensitive personal data unless it is essential to explain the concern.

For a suspected security vulnerability, use the private reporting route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a public conduct report. If the concern involves the repository maintainer, use GitHub's platform reporting mechanisms.

## Handling and investigation
The maintainer will review the report, request additional context when needed, and consider the available evidence. Information will be shared only with people who need it to assess and respond, subject to platform and legal requirements. A report may be investigated without public discussion. Reports made in good faith should not result in retaliation.

The maintainer may remove harmful content, pause a discussion, or restrict participation while reviewing a concern. The person making the report and the person responding may be contacted for clarification. Where the report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's independent platform reporting channels.

## Consequences
Depending on severity, context, and prior behavior, responses may include a private warning, request to correct or remove content, temporary restriction from project spaces, or permanent exclusion from project participation. The maintainer will explain the decision when doing so is appropriate and safe.

## Scope and limitations
This is a project community policy, not a substitute for GitHub's terms, applicable law, or an organization's formal workplace policy. The project does not claim a staffed moderation team or a guaranteed response time.
