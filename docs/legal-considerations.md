# ⚖️ Legal and content considerations

This repository does not make a legal compliance claim. The developer or deployer is responsible for checking applicable privacy, employment, intellectual-property, accessibility, and hosting obligations for the jurisdiction and use case.

## Practical precautions
- Use synthetic or expressly authorized employee records.
- Do not publish real compensation or contact details without appropriate authorization.
- Verify rights to profile photographs, logos, icons, fonts, and other media.
- Keep third-party dependency notices and licenses available.
- Do not claim WCAG compliance without testing and evidence.
- Do not present the app as secure HR software or an approved employee record system.
- Obtain qualified legal/privacy advice before processing real workforce personal information.

The MIT grant for authored code does not override separate rights in third-party assets or sample records.
